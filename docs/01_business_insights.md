# Healthcare Revenue Cycle Analytics
### 1. Project Overview

This project simulates the responsibilities of a Revenue Cycle Data Analyst working for Virginia Rehabilitation Network (VRN), a fictional multi-location outpatient rehabilitation organization operating five clinics across Northern Virginia. The analysis focuses on evaluating healthcare revenue cycle performance, identifying financial inefficiencies, and developing data-driven recommendations to improve reimbursement and collections. The project uses publicly available healthcare datasets alongside clearly documented synthetic operational data to simulate healthcare claims processing, insurance reimbursement, claim denials, and accounts receivable management.

### 2. Business Problem

VRRN leadership has identified potential inefficiencies in its revenue cycle operations, including:

- High insurance claim denial rates.
- Delays in insurance reimbursement.
- Increasing outstanding accounts receivable.
- Inconsistent financial performance across clinic locations.
- Uncollected patient balances.

These issues may negatively affect cash flow, operational efficiency, and financial sustainability.

The organization requires a standardized analytical approach to measure performance, identify areas of concern, and support financial decision-making.

### 3. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

1. Evaluate billed charges, allowed amounts, payments, and outstanding balances.
2. Measure initial insurance claim denial rates across locations and payers.
3. Analyze accounts receivable aging and payment turnaround.
4. Compare revenue cycle performance across five clinic locations.
5. Identify potential causes of reimbursement delays and revenue leakage.
6. Develop interactive executive dashboards and actionable recommendations.

### 4. Scope of Analysis

**Organization:** Virginia Regional Rehabilitation Network (fictional)

**Clinic locations:**
- Alexandria, Virginia
- Arlington, Virginia
- Fairfax, Virginia
- Woodbridge, Virginia
- Manassas, Virginia

**Simulated operating period:** January 1 – December 31, 2025

**Illustrative dataset size:** Approximately 50,000 claims

**Primary analytical tools:** Microsoft Excel, MySQL, Python, and Tableau.

### 5. Data Sources and Assumptions

The project will use public CMS synthetic Medicare claims and publicly available aggregated healthcare claims data where appropriate.

Additional fictional operational records will be generated to support the simulated five-location business scenario.

All synthetic records, assumptions, transformations, and limitations will be documented. Simulated findings will not be presented as actual performance results from CMS or real healthcare providers.

### 6. Expected Deliverables

- Data dictionary and source documentation.
- Excel data exploration workbook.
- MySQL relational database and analytical queries.
- Python data cleaning and exploratory analysis notebooks.
- Tableau executive revenue cycle dashboard.
- Business findings and recommendations report.
- Published GitHub portfolio repository.

### 7. Project Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when the final analysis:

- Produces reproducible and validated revenue cycle KPIs.
- Reconciles claims, adjustments, and payments using documented accounting assumptions.
- Identifies meaningful patterns in denials, reimbursement, and outstanding receivables.
- Compares performance consistently across all five locations.
- Communicates clear, evidence-supported operational recommendations.
